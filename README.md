# 📊 NexMart Power BI Analytics

An interactive **Power BI Business Intelligence dashboard** built to analyze sales, customers, products, inventory, and operational performance for NexMart.

The project transforms transactional data into actionable business insights through interactive dashboards, KPIs, filters, and visual analytics.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to provide a comprehensive view of NexMart's business performance and help identify:

- Sales and revenue trends
- Customer behavior and segmentation
- Regional performance
- Product and category performance
- Inventory and stock levels
- Stockout and reorder requirements
- Profitability and margins
- Order and return patterns

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI**
- **Power Query**
- **DAX**
- **Microsoft Excel / CSV**
- **Data Modeling**
- **Data Visualization**
- **Business Intelligence**

---

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

The project contains four interactive analytical dashboards.

### 1. Executive Overview

Provides a high-level summary of overall business performance.

**Key KPIs:**
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Total Revenue
- Average Order Value
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin

**Visuals include:**
- Revenue & Profit by Year
- Revenue by Region
- Revenue by Category
- Top Products by Profit
- Revenue by State & Region

![Executive Overview](Executive%20Overview.png)

---

### 2. Customer & Sales Analysis

Focuses on customer behavior, sales performance, and order patterns.

**Key KPIs:**
- Total Customers
- Repeat Customer %
- Repeat Customers
- Total Orders
- Average Order Value
- Return Rate %

**Analysis includes:**
- Customers by Segment
- Revenue & Profit by Region
- Orders by Payment Method
- Orders by Status
- Customers by Age Group
- Revenue & Profit by Month

![Customer and Sales Analysis](Customer%20and%20Sales%20Analysis.png)

---

### 3. Product & Operations Analysis

Analyzes product-level revenue and profitability.

**Analysis includes:**
- Total Orders by Order Status
- Revenue by Product Category
- Revenue & Profit by Month
- Product-level Profitability
- Top Products by Profit

![Product and Operations Analysis](Product%20and%20Operations%20Analysis.png)

---

### 4. Inventory & Stock Analysis

Provides visibility into inventory health and stock management.

**Key KPIs:**
- Total Closing Stock
- Total Units Sold
- Total Opening Stock
- Reorder Level

**Analysis includes:**
- Reorder Level vs Closing Stock
- Stockout Count by Product
- Units Sold vs Closing Stock
- Closing Stock Trend

![Inventory and Stock Analysis](Inventory%20and%20Stock%20Analysis.png)
